\# Defense AI Readiness Dashboard (Survey Analytics)



This project visualizes organizational AI readiness (culture, skills, process maturity) using

interactive dashboards and statistical summaries.



\### 🧠 Purpose

\- Measure current adoption barriers

\- Identify workforce training needs

\- Inform realistic AI integration policy



\### 📊 Expected Deliverables

\- Power BI (or Python) dashboards

\- Survey insights and scoring

\- Demographic/workforce maturity comparisons



\### 🔪 Impact

Helps leaders \*\*prioritize training\*\*, \*\*resource allocation\*\*, and \*\*mission-aligned modernization\*\*.
